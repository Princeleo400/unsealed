## 1. What really happened (one paragraph)

**Ronke Bello (née Ajayi), the wedding planner, poisoned Chief Adewale Bankole** with digoxin, her late father's heart medicine, in a gold-rimmed champagne flute she brought herself. In 2021 her father, **Pa Joseph Ajayi**, lost his family land at Abijo, Ajah, to Bankole Homes in **Suit LD/1187/2019**; Ronke testified for him. He died in March 2022. In 2026 she undercut every planner (₦2.5m vs ₦7–9m) to win the Bankole wedding. On the day she wore the bride's family **wine aso-ebi** (ordered "for family photos"), not her team's black uniform. At 19:24 she radioed the DJ to bring the first dance forward from 20:15; at 19:26 **cue 14** blacked out every table. Chief left seat 1 to spray the couple. At 19:28 she set the poured flute at his place (a guest's flash photo caught her back: wine gele, earpiece coil, gold staff wristband). The waiters had a standing instruction never to pour for seat 1, so they skipped him at 19:32. At the toast the MC read the line Ronke had added to his run sheet at 18:40, *"Daddy, today you must drink with us!"*, saw the glass "already there", and Chief drank "just this once". He collapsed at 19:52. Ronke tried to have the high-table glasses cleared at 19:54–19:56; the head waiter and then Dr Eze refused. Police sealed her room at 21:00 with the empty pill bottle (label: *J. AJAYI*) and the twin of the flute inside.

## 2. The cover story the police first believed

A known heart patient had a heart attack at his daughter's wedding. When toxicology found digoxin, suspicion fell on the people *at the high table during the toast* (19:38–19:41), on his doctor (digoxin is a heart drug), and on the family (money, a new will).

## 7. Verdict

- **Who:** Ronke Bello.
- **Method:** digoxin in a champagne flute · **When the glass reached him:** during the blackout, 19:26–19:30 · **Trick:** had the first dance moved forward so the tables went dark.
- **Why:** revenge for her father, who lost his land case to Chief Bankole and died.
- **Proof:** core = flash photo (18) + Room B search (22); third item from support.
